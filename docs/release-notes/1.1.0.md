## English (en-US)

```
After the prayer — a new guided sequence

Follow the adhkar of Hisn al-Muslim after every prayer, step by step. The
twelve invocations come in their traditional order, each with its own
counter: three istighfār, the tasbihāt thirty-three times each up to the
hundred, the sūrahs, Āyat al-Kursī. Tap, and the app moves you on.

Every step shows its Arabic text, its transliteration, its source and its
recitation, so you can read along or listen.

Also in this release:
• The app now runs on iOS 17, not just iOS 18.4 — no need to update your
  iPhone to use it.
• The App Store page finally lists all three languages the app speaks.
  Arabic, French and English were always there; the listing did not say so.
• Fixed: readers on a device set to a language other than Arabic, French or
  English were shown an Arabic interface instead of English.
```

## Notes for whoever submits this

- **App Review and guideline 4.3(a).** Version 1.0 was rejected on
  2026-05-13 as sharing a concept with other Hisn al-Muslim apps. The
  release notes above lead with the guided sequence and its fidelity to the
  source, not with "a counter" — a plain tasbih is the most generic feature
  in this category and describing it that way invites the same comparison.
- **The two conditional steps.** Steps 11 and 12 are recited only after
  Fajr (and Maghrib for step 11). They are labelled in the app and skippable;
  the notes do not mention them, to keep the copy short.
- **Not claimed anywhere:** prayer times, notifications tied to prayer
  times, offline audio, iCloud sync. None of that shipped.
