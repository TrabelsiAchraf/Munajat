After the prayer — a new guided sequence

Follow the adhkar of Hisn al-Muslim after every prayer, step by step. The twelve invocations come in their traditional order, each with its own counter: three istighfar, the tasbihat thirty-three times each up to the hundred, the surahs, Ayat al-Kursi. You tap, the app moves you on.

Every step shows its Arabic text, its transliteration, its source and its recitation, so you can read along or listen.
